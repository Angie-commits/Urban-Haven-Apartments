About Urban Haven Apartments

Urban Haven Apartments has been providing quality housing solutions in Murang'a, Kiharu for over 12 years. We take pride in offering comfortable, modern living spaces that cater to diverse needs and preferences.

Our apartments are designed with your comfort in mind, featuring modern amenities, spacious layouts, and a vibrant community atmosphere. Whether you're looking for a cozy single room or a spacious family apartment, we have the perfect space for you.

At Urban Haven, we believe in creating not just living spaces, but homes where memories are made and communities thrive.
